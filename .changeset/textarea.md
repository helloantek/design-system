Textarea (Sort UI 2.0): `Textarea` on a native `<textarea>` with `label`, `caption`, `error` / `invalid` and `disabled`; at least 64 px tall and growing with its text. `showCount` with `maxLength` or a soft `limit` adds the character count and a progress circle to the label; `resizable` adds a drag handle. Field gains a label row with a trail add-on (`ds-field__label-row`, `ds-field__addon`).
