Calendar (Sort UI 2.0): `Calendar` picks a day (`mode="single"`) or a range (`mode="range"`, `{ from, to }`) with `selected`, `defaultSelected` and `onSelect`, as a popover `panel` or `flat`, with a `label` or dropdown navigation (`captionLayout`), several months (`numberOfMonths`, `orientation`), `min`, `max` and `disabled` days, `weekStartsOn`, `locale`, and `footer` / `aside` slots for presets. The grid is one Tab stop: the arrow keys, Home / End and Page Up / Page Down move across days, weeks, months and years.
