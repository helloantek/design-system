New DecisionInbox (Patterns/Decision Inbox): the open decisions that wait on you, each with two quick answers – what to decide, why now and the agent that asks. An answered one confirms in place with Undo (focus moves to it) and folds away after `dismissAfter`; with nothing open it says so; `folded` makes it one line for a sidebar.
