Toggle Group (Sort UI 2.0): `ToggleGroup` holds `ToggleGroupItem`s, `type` `single` (at most one on) or `multiple`, with `value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange`, in `variant` `default` or `outline`, `spacing` 2 (8 px), 1 (4 px) or 0 (joined in a pill, or a rounded box when vertical, with dividers when outlined), `orientation` and `size`. It is a named group with one Tab stop; the arrow keys, Home and End move between the items.
