The patterns Thinking Loader and Thinking State for Angular, with the React components' markup and behaviour: `<div dsThinkingLoader>` (what it is doing as its content, read out as it changes; `status` thinking, slow with `elapsed` and Stop, done or stopped) and `<div dsThinkingState>` with `<li dsThinkingStep>` steps (`dsThinkingStepIcon`, a spinner while running), `<ul dsThinkingSources>` of `<a dsThinkingSource>` chips with `[more]`, and `<p dsThinkingNote>`; done, it folds to one line that opens back (`[(open)]`).
