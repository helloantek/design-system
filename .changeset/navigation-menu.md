Navigation Menu (Sort UI 2.0): `NavigationMenu` (a nav landmark, `defaultValue`), `NavigationMenuItem` (a trigger with a panel, or a link with `href` and `active`; `disabled`, `width`, `align`, `flush`), `NavigationMenuList` (a column or `columns` grid) and `NavigationMenuLink` (`title`, `description`, `icon`, `iconPlacement`, `active`). Triggers open their panel on click, Arrow Down or resting the mouse; Escape, Tab out and a click outside close it; Arrow Left / Right move between items.
