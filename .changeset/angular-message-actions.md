Popover, Switch and the pattern Message Actions for Angular, with the React components' markup and behaviour: `<div dsPopover>` (its trigger marked `dsPopoverTrigger`; a named non-modal dialog that takes focus, closes on Escape, a click outside or focus leaving, and on a `dsPopoverClose` button; `side`, `align`, `width`, `[(open)]`; `dsPopoverActions`), `<ds-switch>` (a native checkbox with `role="switch"`, with a label, caption or error; `[(checked)]`, Reactive Forms, ngModel and Signal Forms) and `<div dsMessageActions>` (Copy, Regenerate with versions, thumbs up and down with what was wrong in a Popover, Read aloud).
