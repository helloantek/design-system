Hover Card (Sort UI 2.0): `HoverCard` previews what is behind a link in a 288 px card (`width`) while the pointer rests on it (`openDelay`, 700 ms) or the link has keyboard focus. It stays while the pointer or focus is on the link or the card, closes `closeDelay` (300 ms) after the pointer leaves, and Escape closes it; `side` and `align` place it 4 px from the trigger.
