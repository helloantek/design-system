Accordion (Sort UI 2.0): `Accordion` (`type` single or multiple, `value` / `defaultValue` / `onValueChange`, `collapsible`, `variant` card / flush / separated, `headingLevel`), `AccordionItem` (`disabled`, `divider`), `AccordionTrigger` (`icon`) and `AccordionContent`. Triggers are buttons in headings with `aria-expanded`; panels are labelled regions; Up / Down, Home and End move between triggers.
