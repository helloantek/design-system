Tabs (Sort UI 2.0): `Tabs` holds the selected `value` (`defaultValue`, `onValueChange`), `variant` `default`, `secondary` or `line`, `size` `default` or `lg` and `orientation`; `TabsList` is the tablist (`fullWidth` stretches it), `TabsTrigger` a tab with a label and optional `icon`, `count`, `eyebrow` or `description`, `TabsContent` its panel. One Tab stop: the arrow keys, Home and End move between the tabs and select them, skipping disabled ones.
