New ApprovalGates (Patterns/Approval Gates): the approvals of one agent run as a stack. A summary of what waits, what is decided and where the run is; `ApprovalGate`, the gate to decide now with the next ones peeking out under it – Approve (A), Reject (R) or Change it first, which turns the arguments into a field; a list of decided gates that open to their reason; and the stopped and all-decided states.
