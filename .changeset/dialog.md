Dialog (Sort UI 2.0): `Dialog` opens a modal window from its `trigger` with a `title`, a `description`, the Close button (`closeButton`), any content and a `footer`; `DialogClose` makes a button close it. Focus moves inside and stays there; Escape, Close or a click on the backdrop closes it and returns focus. It is 448 px wide (`width`), the content scrolls past the viewport while the header and footer stay, `footerVariant="muted"` fills the footer and `bodyPadding="flush"` drops the content's padding.
