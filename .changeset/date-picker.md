Date Picker (Sort UI 2.0): `DatePicker` is a button (or, with `input`, a text input with a calendar button) that opens a Calendar 4 px below for one date or a `mode="range"`, with `value`, `defaultValue`, `onValueChange`, `placeholder`, `format`, `showIcon`, `name` (YYYY-MM-DD) and the Calendar's limits and navigation. Focus moves into the calendar and back; Escape and outside clicks close it; inside a Field it takes the label, description and error.
