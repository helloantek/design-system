New ToolCall and ToolCallGroup (Patterns/Tool Call Card): an agent's tool call in one line – the tool, what it does, its status and time – that opens to the arguments and the result, each with Copy; a failed call opens with the error, Retry and Copy error. A group holds one turn's calls: "Using tools…" while running, then a card that sums them up and folds to one line.
