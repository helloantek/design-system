Bubble and AI Chat Composer for Angular, with the React components' markup and behaviour: `<div dsBubble>` and `<div dsBubbleGroup>` (variants, alignment, reactions on a corner, a long message with Show more) and `<div dsAIChatComposer>` (the prompt grows with its text and turns into a column when it wraps, Enter sends, /commands are tinted, Expand, dictating and transcribing; content marked `dsComposerLeading`, `dsComposerBadges`, `dsComposerModel`, `dsComposerHeader`), with `<mark dsAIChatComposerToken>`. Model Picker and Thinking Loader get their stories in the composer and in a chat.
