New ReviewCenter (Patterns/Review Center): one place to review what the agents want to do. Progress and the last decision with Undo (Z) on top; the queue with filters on the left (`ReviewCenterQueueItem`); the item to decide on the right (`ReviewCenterItem`) with why, the fields that matter (an unusual value marked), attachments and single-key actions – Skip (S), Reject (R), Edit (E), Approve (A). Reject asks why with a number per reason; `ReviewCenterDone` when all are reviewed.
