Badge has a Tint style, Sort UI 2.0's Badge Type=Tint: `variant="tint"` draws the palette tint with its text colour in every colour, plus `amber` and `teal` (Tint only); neutral is 2.0's Secondary. Sizes keep their heights (sm 20 px, lg 24 px) with 2.0's spacing to the text. The patterns drawn on Sort UI 2.0 use it for risk, status and count labels.
