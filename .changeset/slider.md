Slider (Sort UI 2.0): `Slider` picks a number, or a range with `[from, to]`, between `min` and `max` in `step`s (`value`, `defaultValue`, `onValueChange`, `onValueCommit`), horizontal or vertical, optionally `disabled`, with an optional `label` and `valueText` row and a `name` for forms. `variant="block"` (a 24 px track with 20 × 24 thumbs, optional step `marks` and a colour `thumbDot`) and `variant="scrubber"` (a 32 px bar with the label and value inside) give the demos' looks. Each thumb is a `role="slider"`: the arrow keys, Page Up / Page Down, Home and End move it, and a click or drag on the track moves the nearest thumb.
