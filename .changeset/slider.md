Slider (Sort UI 2.0): `Slider` picks a number, or a range with `[from, to]`, between `min` and `max` in `step`s (`value`, `defaultValue`, `onValueChange`, `onValueCommit`), horizontal or vertical, optionally `disabled`, with an optional `label` and `valueText` row and a `name` for forms. Each thumb is a `role="slider"`: the arrow keys, Page Up / Page Down, Home and End move it, and a click or drag on the track moves the nearest thumb.
