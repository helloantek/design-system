Drawer (Sort UI 2.0): `Drawer` opens a rounded modal panel that floats 8 px from the bottom (`direction="down"`, the default), the top (`up`) or the end edge (`side`), with a swipe handle (`swipeHandle`) that closes it when dragged towards the edge, a `title`, a `description`, any content and a `footer` whose buttons stack full-width; `DrawerClose` makes a button close it. Down and up are 664 px wide with a centred heading, side is full-height and 384 px wide (`width`). Focus stays inside; Escape or a click on the backdrop closes it and returns focus. `closeButton` adds a Close button, which Figma's Drawer does not have.
