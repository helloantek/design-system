The patterns Decision Card and Approval Gates for Angular, with the React components' markup and behaviour: `<section dsDecisionCard>` (numbered options and tiles, a number key answers, your own words in the last row, a pager, Later and the folded line) and `<section dsApprovalGates>` with `<div dsApprovalGate>` (A approves, R rejects, Change it edits the arguments first; Esc cancels, Enter approves with changes), `dsApprovalGatesStopped`, `dsApprovalGatesDone`, `dsApprovalGatesDecided` and `<li dsApprovalGatesDecidedItem>`. Input passes `aria-keyshortcuts` to its native input.
