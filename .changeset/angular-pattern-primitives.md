Kbd, Spinner, Toggle, Toggle Group and Avatar for Angular, with the React components' markup and behaviour: `<kbd dsKbd>` and `<kbd dsKbdGroup>` (`variant`, `size`, a spoken `label` for symbols), `<span dsSpinner>` (`variant`, `size`, `label` or `decorative`), `<button dsToggle>` (`variant`, `size`, `[(pressed)]`, icon slots), `<div dsToggleGroup>` with `<button dsToggle value="…">` items (`type` single or multiple, `[(value)]`, `variant`, `size`, `spacing`, `orientation`, `disabled`; one Tab stop and the arrow keys), and `<span dsAvatar>` (`src`, `initials`, `name`, `size`, `shape`, `ring`, a fallback when the picture fails) in `<span dsAvatarGroup>` (`size`, `total` for "+N").
