Stepper for Angular, with the React component's markup: `<ol dsStepper>` (`orientation`, `variant` indicator or bar, and `steps` with `current`) or `<li dsStepperItem>` children (`state`, `label`, `caption`, `number`, `showLine`, `statusLabel`), numbered by their place. The current step is `aria-current="step"`; done and failed steps say so to screen readers.
