New Checkbox (Sort UI 2.0) on a native checkbox: Checked, Focus, Disabled, Invalid and indeterminate; with `label`, `caption`, `error` and `control` (start, end) it renders the horizontal Field, `card` makes it a choice card, and `CheckboxGroup` puts checkboxes under a legend. Styles: `ds-checkbox*` in `checkbox.css`; `ds-field--horizontal`, `ds-field__control`, `ds-field__content`, `ds-field--card` and `ds-fieldset*` in `input.css`.
