Popover (Sort UI 2.0): `Popover` opens a 288 px non-modal panel from its `trigger`, with a `title`, a `description` and any content; `PopoverActions` lines up its buttons and `PopoverClose` makes one close it. `side` (`bottom`, `top`, `left`, `right`) and `align` (`center`, `start`, `end`) place it 4 px from the trigger; `width` changes its width. Focus moves into it, Escape closes it and returns focus to the trigger, a click outside closes it.
