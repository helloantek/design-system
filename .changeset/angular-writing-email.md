The patterns Writing Assistant and Email Draft for Angular, with the React components' markup and behaviour: `<section dsWritingAssistant>` (the selection, suggestions, the reply streaming in with Stop, then Replace with Enter, Insert below and Copy; a composer marked `dsWritingAssistantComposer`) and `<section dsEmailDraft>` (the recipient Select, the attachment, the text to edit in place, the tone, rewriting, Send with ⌘↵ or Discard, then Undo for a few seconds or Restore). Select takes `aria-labelledby`.
