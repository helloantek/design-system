Sheet (Sort UI 2.0): `Sheet` opens a modal panel along an edge of the screen (`side`: `right`, `left`, `top`, `bottom`) with a `title`, a `description`, the Close button, any content and a `footer` whose buttons stack full-width; `SheetClose` makes a button close it. Left and right sheets are full-height and 384 px wide (`width`), top and bottom full-width. It slides in from its edge, keeps focus inside, and Escape, Close or a click on the backdrop closes it and returns focus. Dialog and Sheet now share their modal code; Dialog's behaviour is unchanged.
