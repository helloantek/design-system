Menubar (Sort UI 2.0): `Menubar` (a `role="menubar"` bar, `defaultValue`) and `MenubarMenu` (`label`, `value`, `align`, `disabled`), each opening a Dropdown Menu 8 px below its 26 px trigger. One Tab stop; Arrow Left / Right move between triggers and, with a menu open, switch menus, as does the pointer; Enter, Space and Arrow Down open a menu on its first item; Escape and a click outside close it.
